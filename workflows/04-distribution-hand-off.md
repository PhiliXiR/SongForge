# Workflow 04 - Distribution Hand-off

## Goal

Prepare approved, packaged songs for manual or semi-manual distributor submission.

## MVP Recommendation

Do not auto-submit to distributors in v1.

Instead:
- mark the record `queued_for_distribution`
- verify required fields exist
- notify operator that package is ready

## Required Checks

- human approved
- title finalized
- metadata present
- artwork present
- audio file present
- monetization decision recorded
